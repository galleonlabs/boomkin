# Minara and the open-source Boomkin product

Research date: 2 October 2026. This is a source-based product assessment, not a live Minara account test, financial recommendation, performance comparison, or feature-parity claim.

## Assessment

Boomkin should make a useful DeFi research desk available in a user-owned agent environment: a direct install path, public data that produces a first result, protocol-specific jobs, and portable evidence that another person can inspect. That is a substantial product improvement. Calling a collection of procedures an equivalent to Minara's wallet, strategy engine, marketplace, or Autopilot would obscure the engineering still required.

Minara's most transferable strength is continuity between a question, cited observations, a reviewable decision, and a later check on its outcome. Its product also contains hosted infrastructure that is not supplied by its open-source skills or CLI. The research-to-monitoring loop is the best immediate target for Boomkin; managed execution and a strategy engine should be assessed as separate integrations.

## Method and source boundaries

Reviewed Minara's [documentation index](https://minara.ai/docs/llms.txt), homepage, product pages, 27 detailed documentation pages, open CLI, and open skills. Public Markdown URLs were inconsistent during this review; extensionless GitBook HTML pages were readable. No account was created, model queried, wallet connected, subscription purchased, order submitted, or provider credential accessed.

Public visual review found a sparse black-and-white opening with an oversized serif headline, a terminal-like background, and denser financial panels lower down. This report's product behavior findings come from published documentation and source; they do not establish that every marketed interaction works for a new account.

## What Minara actually combines

### 1. A short account-to-task path

Minara's documented onboarding is Google or email verification, then its chat interface. Spot and venue-specific perps wallets are created automatically. The user does not first choose a runtime, assemble skill packs, or configure an RPC. That explains much of the perceived completeness. [Account creation](https://minara.ai/docs/get-started/activate-your-account)

The wallet selector determines venue, balance, margin, and order context. Each perps wallet belongs to either Lighter or Hyperliquid, and the deposit guide requires USDC on Arbitrum. Transfers, on-ramp availability, and settlement have their own constraints. A unified interface does not make all assets, venues, and deposits interchangeable. [Deposit and wallet management](https://minara.ai/docs/get-started/deposit)

**Boomkin implication:** keep its isolated native Hermes profile and explicit provider choice, but shorten time to a useful public result. Offer a bounded task before any wallet connection, and say which additional inputs enable deeper jobs.

### 2. Research outputs with an appropriate level of effort

The documented chat modes are ordinary chat, Deep Research, and Data Visualization. Deep Research decomposes a question, queries several sources, cross-validates, and produces a structured cited report; visualization returns tables or charts. The docs describe downloadable PDF/Word reports and file inputs. These are output contracts, not merely longer prompts. [Research modes](https://minara.ai/docs/chat/deep-research)

The chat product page emphasizes exact-source links, timestamped report windows, source disagreements, exports, and the ability to make a recurring job from a question. Those claims are useful acceptance criteria for an open product, even where their hosted implementation has not been independently tested here. [Chat product](https://minara.ai/product/chat)

**Boomkin implication:** distinguish a quick observation from a research brief. A brief should retain machine-readable evidence, exact query parameters, identity, units, observation time, retrieval time, missing fields, assumptions, and links. A chart must be derived from the same recorded numbers.

### 3. Discovery that gives the user a question worth asking

The homepage leads with concrete tasks, then shows work steps and a result: market selection, a strategy comparison, or an account overview. Crypto Picks has a narrower documented purpose: short-horizon bullish/bearish watchlists, initial tracking price, current evidence, dynamic targets, and removal history. It is not fundamental valuation. [Homepage](https://minara.ai/home), [Crypto Picks](https://minara.ai/docs/chat/crypto-picks)

**Boomkin implication:** make the landing page task-led. Show a source-backed market brief, yield comparison, position inspection, and written thesis review. A synthetic product example must carry that label. A public sample containing current prices must carry its captured timestamp and sources.

### 4. A structured Copilot handoff

Copilot evaluates multi-timeframe price action, liquidity, funding, and open interest. Its documented result is long, short, or neutral with entry, take-profit, stop-loss, and rationale. The user reviews the plan and chooses whether to place the venue-specific order. The underlying exchanges supply pricing and liquidity; Minara presents the interface and execution layer. [Trading Copilot](https://minara.ai/docs/trade/trading-copilot)

Galleon's existing Hyperliquid ticket procedure is already more explicit about network, account mode, DEX, instrument IDs, precision, fees, margin, client order IDs, expiry, reduce-only protection, partial fills, and reconciliation. This should be presented as a concrete strength, without implying that installing the procedure establishes authenticated execution. [Baseline Hyperliquid plan](https://github.com/galleonlabs/crypto-defi-skills/blob/96c46d1ef427456816b0268ffca8732bd9415559/packages/hyperliquid/skills/hyperliquid-plan/SKILL.md)

### 5. A real strategy and execution stack

Strategy Studio uses Pine Runtime, Minara's TypeScript DSL. It has two distinct OHLC backtest models: time-series strategies make candle-based decisions and use lower-timeframe candles for certain intrabar triggers; cross-sectional strategies rank a universe and rebalance at candle close. The docs say fees, slippage, and perpetual funding are modeled, and identify liquidity, latency, rejection, leverage, overfitting, and cold-start bias as limits. [Strategy Studio](https://minara.ai/docs/trade/strategy-studio), [Backtesting FAQ](https://minara.ai/docs/support/faq/strategy-studio/backtesting)

Autopilot runs selected strategies within a specified asset scope and wallet. Its documented rules include canceling existing open orders on start, cross-margin eligibility, reserved risk for eligible existing positions, strategy-specific constraints, protection orders, drawdown stops, and handling manual overrides. These are runtime state and enforcement requirements, not a generic cron prompt. [Autopilot](https://minara.ai/docs/trade/trading-autopilot)

**Boomkin implication:** a strategy template may help draft or review an idea. It cannot honestly be marketed as a backtesting engine, paper trading, or autonomous execution without the corresponding executable integration and evidence.

### 6. Recurring workflows and retained reasoning

Minara's visual Workflow surface combines triggers, conditions, AI queries, notifications, trades, and generated code. Deployed workflows cannot be edited in place according to the current guide; credit exhaustion pauses them. Its visual builder is separate from the core agent loop. [Workflow](https://minara.ai/docs/workflow/agentic-workflow)

The newer harness docs describe recurring research more carefully: subject, sources, cadence, output, notification rule, observable failure, and quiet runs until useful change. Its thesis tracker records the asset, thesis, invalidation conditions, evidence standard, frequency, and notification rule; the first run uses cited evidence and distinguishes invalidation from ordinary volatility. The docs explicitly do not promise an immutable trade journal. [Automation boundaries](https://minara.ai/docs/agent/build-and-extend/hooks-cron-delegation), [Thesis tracker](https://minara.ai/docs/agent/start-here/use-cases/thesis-tracker)

**Boomkin implication:** use native Hermes scheduling and memory. Produce a reviewable monitoring specification before creating an actual job. Record successful scheduling and a successful first run separately. A saved template is not a running monitor.

## Architecture and the open-source boundary

Minara separates model reasoning from context assembly, tool access, memory, workspace execution, and authorization. Tools are exposed selectively, their results are observations, and memory cannot create financial permission. Its financial docs distinguish research, preview, submission, confirmation, fill, and account state. Those are sound architecture principles to preserve through Hermes. [Model and harness](https://minara.ai/docs/agent/start-here/model-and-harness), [Context and tools](https://minara.ai/docs/agent/core-concepts/context-and-tools), [Financial authorization](https://minara.ai/docs/agent/operate-safely/financial-authorization)

The public CLI is an API client. Its [request implementation](https://github.com/Minara-AI/minara-cli/blob/25e64ae6f14561dddabb51c1edbcd141866c4456/src/api/client.ts) uses a configured hosted API, and [chat](https://github.com/Minara-AI/minara-cli/blob/25e64ae6f14561dddabb51c1edbcd141866c4456/src/api/chat.ts) streams authenticated requests to the service. The open [Minara skill](https://github.com/Minara-AI/minara-skills/blob/8badf3bcaff1592b79128dcea4ea164246909580/skills/minara/SKILL.md) routes tasks through that CLI. Source availability for the client does not establish source availability or self-hostability for the data layer, signing service, strategy engine, or marketplace.

Minara calls its wallets non-custodial while explicitly stating that it generates and operates the controlling key on the user's behalf using AWS KMS. It describes EIP-7702 delegation, stablecoin gas payment, revocation, and key export. This review does not assess the legal custody classification or independently audit those claims. The technical distinction relevant to Boomkin is that Minara operates a managed signing service; Boomkin's setup does not. [Wallet security](https://minara.ai/docs/technology/wallet-security)

The tool-stack docs describe licensed/private data alongside public providers. Some pages claim more than 40 providers and others more than 50. That does not imply that an open replacement can redistribute every feed. Open code, public data, freely available data, and provider access are separate properties. [Tool stack](https://minara.ai/docs/technology/tools-integration), [About](https://minara.ai/docs)

DMind has public model artifacts, but provider documentation is not a reason to pin an untested local model. The model-family guide and Hugging Face display differ on some parameter counts. Evaluate the specific artifact's license, chat template, tool calling, requirements, and benchmark behavior before presenting it as a supported model. Hermes's existing model choice remains the appropriate boundary. [Model family](https://minara.ai/docs/technology/dmind/model-family), [Published DMind artifacts](https://huggingface.co/DMindAI)

## Priorities with observable acceptance criteria

### P0: Make public research a working entry point

A fresh user should be able to get a bounded, keyless result without funding a wallet. Market and yield requests need explicit inputs, bounded results, source time, retrieval time, units, quality/freshness policy, missing fields, and clear rate-limit errors. Save raw or normalized evidence beside a concise Markdown brief. Treat outages and stale data as visible gaps.

**Acceptance:** isolated-install test; representative public-provider read; malformed, empty, stale, and rate-limited fixtures; artifacts agree numerically; success never silently changes the provider or turns missing data into zero.

### P0: Ship the public website as a product guide

Lead with one clear promise, one install action, and a representative work product. Use a structured research example to teach evidence and next steps, then show actual jobs and independent packs. Give documentation a deliberate hierarchy: start, research, jobs, connections, artifacts, updates, contribution. Source content from the catalog where possible so counts and identifiers do not drift.

**Acceptance:** mobile and desktop render checks; working install-copy control; keyboard navigation; no overflow; readable contrast; functioning links; current catalog counts; public demo clearly labeled; deployment revision and serving page verified.

### P1: Make research and thesis monitoring portable procedures

A research brief needs a decision, horizon, universe, source plan, observations, contrary evidence, uncertainty, and the smallest next check. A thesis tracker needs written invalidation criteria, evidence quality, cadence, saved state location, and a meaningful-change notification rule. Provider failure is a failed check, not an invalidated thesis.

**Acceptance:** scenarios for stale sources, shared upstreams, conflicting observations, ordinary volatility, true invalidation, missing data, and a recurrence that makes no unauthorized trade or notification. Validate the pinned Hermes scheduler contract before writing exact invocation instructions.

### P1: Publish evaluation evidence that describes what was tested

The existing corpus checks validate packaging, references, routing fixtures, and deterministic helpers. They should not become a marketing model score. Add agent runs only with explicit model/runtime revisions, fixed fixtures, recorded outputs, decision-level grading, and honest scope.

**Acceptance:** an independent reader can rerun the cases and distinguish structural tests, model behavior, public-provider smoke, authenticated capabilities, and financial settlement.

### P2: Choose optional maintained engines for the true runtime gaps

Backtesting and automated strategies deserve an upstream integration decision. Evaluate a maintained engine against historical coverage, costs, account semantics, precision, execution recovery, license, and reproducible paper behavior. Avoid adding a homemade engine merely to decorate the website.

**Acceptance:** a pinned executable contract, safe backtest/paper run, independent artifact review, and clear engine/venue limits before making product claims.

## Presentation rules

- Present concrete jobs and outputs before pack inventory.
- Demonstrate the question, observed work, evidence, and next decision in one coherent example.
- Put identity, time, source, and risk beside the metric they qualify.
- Use a restrained financial-workspace visual language; do not reuse Minara's assets, copy, reported returns, customer logos, or testimonials.
- Label illustrative data as illustrative. Never use simulated profit, speed, “always on,” or provider counts as proof.
- Separate runtime support, installable procedures, connected read access, authenticated execution, and terminal settlement.
- Explain optional provider costs and model requirements at the point they matter.
- Maintain an honest capability boundary on the site. A local open-source product can be compelling on its own merits.

## Decisions this research supports

Use Hermes for the operating layer. Keep the independent pack architecture. Add a useful public research path and reusable evidence artifacts. Make the website demonstrate those outcomes. Add a portable thesis/research procedure where it improves actual task quality. Treat strategy engines, hosted services, managed signing, and execution enforcement as separate engineering contracts.

The reference bar is the clarity and completeness of Minara's user journey. The open-source implementation should earn its own capability claims through reproducible results.
