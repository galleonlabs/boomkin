A local daily spot simulator, using the same engine as the strategy skill. Use the dated Bitcoin series, try synthetic prices or bring your own dataset. Your uploaded data stays in this browser.

Strategy settings

10 basis points = 0.10%. Costs apply to each simulated trade.

Execution assumptions

Long-only fractional spot. Signals use observations already seen and execute at the next daily mark. Fixed adverse slippage and fees apply. Final holdings stay marked, without forced liquidation. The benchmark has the same dates, cash flows and costs.

Aggregated daily prices are research marks. They do not model order books, intraday stops, liquidity, funding, taxes or venue fills.
